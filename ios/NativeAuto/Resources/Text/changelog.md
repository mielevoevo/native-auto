# What's new in Native Auto for iPhone

## 0.5 (5) · 2026-11-17
- Media card: play, pause and skip for Apple Music with the track and its art; the output picker (AirPods, helmet, speaker) and the volume for whatever plays. Spotify and other apps can't be controlled by another app on iOS.
- Call card: while a call rings or is on, it takes the media card's place (never a fullscreen card) with the call's time; over a fullscreen card it's a small pill at the top. Answer and end the call with the phone or the headset.
- Settings in pages: Trip and Garage, Display, Layout, Navigation, Audio, Backup, Privacy and data.
- Auto-start: a trip begins by itself once you ride above 15 km/h for the time you pick (off unless you turn it on).
- The route line can have its own colour (Settings, Display).
- First-run pages on a new install, this list of changes, and the Native Auto icon.
- Share a place from Google Maps straight to Native Auto.

## 0.4 (4) · 2026-11-17
- Trips are saved when they end: the history by day, with a map of each route, the addresses, GPX export and delete.
- Backup and import in the same file as the Android app, so trips and the Garage move between the phones.
- Garage: bikes, odometer, fuel with range and fill-ups, prices, services, and the Garage card on the dashboard.

## 0.3 (3) · 2026-11-17
- Map with OpenFreeMap, follow camera and recentre; navigation with motorcycle routing, search, voice and the Navigation card; Google Maps links.

## 0.2 (2) · 2026-11-16
- The dashboard: layouts, drag to swap, maximize and fullscreen, the speed and trip log faces, the clock, themes and accent colours, portrait.

## 0.1 (1) · 2026-11-16
- First build: the trip core and its tests.
